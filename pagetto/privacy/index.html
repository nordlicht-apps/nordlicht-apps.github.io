Pagetto Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Pagetto (the "app") handles information. The app is published by Nordlicht Apps Studio ("we", "us", "the developer").

In short

  • The app has no account, no ads and no tracking.
  • Your PDF files and images are processed on your phone only. We do not receive them.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Files you open

You choose the PDF files and images you want to work with using Android's file picker, or by sharing a file to the app. The app makes temporary working copies inside its private storage to merge, split, reorganize, compress, redact or sign them. These copies are deleted when the app cleans up its cache or when you uninstall it. The results are saved only to the location you choose or shared through the share sheet you open. The developer has no access to your files.

What the app does not do

  • It does not require an account or a login.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not use crash-reporting services.
  • It does not upload your files, signatures or any other content to us or to any third party.
  • It does not request access to all files on your phone.

Signature image

If you draw a signature, it is kept only on your device and is placed on a PDF as an image when you choose. It is not a qualified electronic signature under EU law (eIDAS).

Purchases

Most tools are free. Some tools (redaction, signature image, batch processing) are unlocked with a one-time purchase. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks on your device, through Google Play, whether you have a valid purchase. Google's handling of your data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

For this reason, the app includes the Google Play Billing library, which uses the internet connection of your phone to communicate with Google Play. The app does not use the internet for anything else.

Retention and deletion

The app keeps only its settings (for example the language) and whether you have unlocked the paid tools. When you uninstall the app, all data stored by the app is removed. Files you saved yourself are not removed, because they are stored in a location you chose.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal data, there is generally no personal data about you for us to access, correct or delete. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom and have a question about your rights under data protection law, you can contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app changes. The date at the top shows when the policy was last updated. The current version is always available at this address.
