Abovio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Abovio (the "app") handles information. The app is published by Nordlicht Apps Studio ("we", "us", "the developer").

In short

  • The app has no account, no ads and no tracking.
  • The subscription information you enter stays on your phone. We do not receive it.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Information you enter

You can add subscriptions to the app: a name, an amount, a currency, a category, a billing cycle and the next renewal date. You can also choose app settings such as the language, the reminder time, the reminder days and your own exchange rates. All of this is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. The developer has no access to it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not require an account or a login, and it does not ask for your bank details or any bank login.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not use crash-reporting services.
  • It does not send your subscription data to us or to any third party.
  • It does not download exchange rates. Exchange rates are entered by you.

Notifications

The app can show reminders before a subscription renews. The reminders are created on your device from the data stored on your device. Android may ask for your permission to show notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

Purchases

The app can be used for free with a limited number of subscriptions. More subscriptions can be unlocked with a monthly plan, a yearly plan or a one-time purchase. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks on your device, through Google Play, whether you have a valid purchase. Google's handling of your data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

For this reason, the app includes the Google Play Billing library, which uses the internet connection of your phone to communicate with Google Play. The app does not use the internet for anything else.

Backups

The app can save a backup of your data to a file and restore it from a file. You choose where the file is saved using Android's file picker. The app does not upload the file anywhere. If you choose a location that is managed by another service, for example a cloud storage provider, that service's terms and privacy policy apply to the file. The app also excludes its data from Android's automatic cloud backup.

Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete a subscription inside the app at any time. When you uninstall the app, all data stored by the app is removed from your device. Backup files that you saved yourself are not removed, because they are stored in a location you chose.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal data, there is generally no personal data about you for us to access, correct or delete. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom and have a question about your rights under data protection law, you can contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app changes. The date at the top shows when the policy was last updated. The current version is always available at this address.
